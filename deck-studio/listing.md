Presentations from a sentence, a text or a file. Tell your Bot what the talk is about, who is watching and how long it is, or give it your notes or a document, and it writes the story and every slide. Deck studio designs them.

**What you get**

- Twelve themes (editorial, dark tech, Swiss, forest, playful, elegant and more) and your own brand from the Logo designer. One click changes the look of every slide.
- Seventeen layouts: title, sections, points, text and picture, cards, big numbers, charts, quotes, timelines, steps, comparisons and pricing, tables, people, full pictures, statements and a closing slide.
- Pictures beside any slide or behind it, with zoom, focus and a shade, plus icons and real charts (bar, line, area, pie, donut).
- Transitions between slides: fade, slide, push, zoom, rise and flip.

**Edit it your way**

- Click any text on a slide and type. Drag slides to reorder them, drop a picture onto a slide, switch its layout, hide a backup slide.
- Or ask the Bot, right beside the deck: "shorter", "make this a chart", "find a photo for this slide". The slide changes in front of you, with Undo.

**Present and share**

- Present full screen, with speaker notes only you see.
- Export a PowerPoint that stays editable (real text, charts and tables), a PDF that looks exactly like the slides, or a single web page that presents itself or scrolls as a one-page site.
- Publish that page as a website with the Sites app.

**How to start:** install it, then ask your Bot: *"Make me a 10-minute pitch deck about ..."*, or open Deck studio from Apps and describe the deck there.

## FAQ

### Do I need PowerPoint or Keynote?
No. You edit, present and export in Deck studio. The PowerPoint file is for when you, or someone you send it to, wants to keep working in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides.

### Is the PowerPoint file really editable?
Yes. Text stays text with its font, size and colour, and charts and tables are native PowerPoint charts and tables. Backgrounds and pictures come in as images. The theme's fonts are saved next to the file, so install them to see it exactly as designed.

### Where do the pictures come from?
Your own pictures (upload them or drop them onto a slide), or your Bot finds one for a slide on free photo sites such as Unsplash, Pexels and Wikimedia Commons and notes where it came from. A deck also looks good with no pictures at all: the themes bring their own decoration.

### Can I use my company's colours and logo?
Yes. Make your brand in the Logo designer app and pick it as the theme, light or dark. The logo goes on the slides by itself.

### Does it work with a local model?
Yes. The deck is written by whatever model your Bot uses; a stronger model writes a better story. The design, editing, presenting and exports all happen in the app on your computer.

### Where are my decks kept?
In your workspace, in the `decks` folder: one folder per deck with its `deck.json` and its pictures, and every export next to them. They are plain files you can read, back up or move. The app itself sends nothing anywhere: with a cloud model, what you ask your Bot and the deck's text go to that model's provider, as with anything else you ask your Bot.
