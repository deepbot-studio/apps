# DeepBot apps

Apps for [DeepBot](https://deepbot.studio). Each app is in its own folder, with its own README and
its own licence, and each is tagged `<app>-v<version>`.

- **[Tutor](tutor/)** - a personal tutor for children and adults: courses planned for one learner,
  interactive lessons with graphs and simulations, exercises checked step by step, flashcards on a
  spaced-repetition schedule and a progress page. MIT.
- **[Deck studio](deck-studio/)** - presentations from a sentence, a text or a file: the Bot writes the
  story and every slide, the app designs them in twelve themes and seventeen layouts, and you edit any
  text in place and export an editable PowerPoint, a PDF or a web page. MIT.
- **[Chess](chess/)** - chess with your Bot at the table: a wooden 3D board, five levels, a clock,
  hints and takebacks, six courses of lessons, thousands of rated puzzles, and a review of every game
  with the Bot explaining any move beside the board. MIT.

More apps arrive here as they are published.
