A personal tutor for children and adults. Tell your Bot what someone needs to learn ("quadratic functions for my daughter, exam in three weeks", "fractions for an 8-year-old", "statistics for my job") and it plans a course for exactly that learner, then teaches it in a page made for learning.

**What a lesson has**

- Clear explanations with real maths notation
- Worked examples revealed one step at a time
- Graphs with sliders, and simulations (projectile, pendulum, spring, waves, circuits, orbits, population, supply and demand, dice, fractions)
- Nine kinds of questions, checked on the spot, with hints
- Exercises solved step by step, the way a teacher would check them
- Flashcards on a spaced-repetition schedule
- Stars, streaks and a progress page

A playful look for children and a calm one for adults. The learner can ask the tutor about any lesson at any time, right next to it.

**How to start:** install it, then ask your Bot: *"Make me a course on ..."*.

## FAQ

### Does it work with a local model?
Yes. The lessons are written by whatever model your Bot uses; a stronger model writes better lessons. With a local model, everything stays on your computer.

### Can several children use it?
Yes. Each course is made for one learner, so make one course per child.

### Where are the courses kept?
In your workspace, in the `tutor` folder, as plain files you can read, edit or back up. Progress (scores, streaks, flashcards) is kept by DeepBot on your computer. The app itself sends nothing anywhere: lessons are written by your Bot's model, so with a cloud model your requests and the lesson text go to that model's provider, as with anything else you ask your Bot.
